# What's New

WellBee is continually evolving, with improvements, new features and refinements designed to make managing your family's health easier.

This page keeps a record of what's changed with each release, so you can stay up to date with the latest additions and improvements.

<br>

### `Latest Update`
*22 October 2026*

Small changes and improvements to make WellBee easier to use:

• **Navigation** — Returned to Apple's native tab bar for a more familiar navigation experience.  
• **Entry Selection** — Entry Type Selection is now a dedicated tab bar item, with redesigned buttons to make more options visible on screen.  
• **Family Profiles** — Moved Growth Metric Details into individual user profiles and redesigned the User Overview card.  
• **History View** — Redesigned action buttons for a more consistent experience.  
• **Onboarding** — Redesigned the entry flow to improve the initial experience.

<br>

### `Version 1.3.0`
*19 October 2026*

**Growth Metric History is here!**

• **Dedicated History Views** — Separate history views for height, weight and head circumference.  
• **Compact Charts** — Quickly review changes across your most recent growth metric entries.  
• **Individual User Overviews** — Each family member now has their own overview section, accessible from their User Details View.  
• Removed the previous family overview section.  
• Additional visual refinements and optimisations.

<br>

### `Version 1.2.0`
*12 October 2026*

**Introducing Temperature History!**

• **Temperature History View** — Tap an abnormal temperature reading from the Home View to access a dedicated history page.  
• **Temperature Status** — View the current temperature status and other important summary information.  
• **Five-Day Charts** — Review temperature readings over the most recent five days.  
• **Complete History** — Browse all historical temperature entries in a chronological timeline.  
• **Quick Updates** — Quickly add a new temperature reading directly from the history view.  
• Additional optimisations throughout the app.

### `Initial Release`

# **Version 1.0**
*1 September 2026*

WellBee is here!

WellBee helps you organise and track the health of your entire family — all in one place.

Create individual profiles and keep a clear, structured history of appointments, illnesses, treatments and everyday health observations.

With WellBee you can manage:

• **Appointments** — Keep a complete history of medical, dental or veterinary visits, including scans, prescriptions and notes.  
• **Diagnoses** — Track ongoing or past conditions across family members.  
• **Growth Metrics** — Monitor height, weight and head circumference over time.  
• **Injuries** — Log bumps, cuts and incidents alongside treatments given at home.  
• **Medications** — Record medication details, dosage and administration times.  
• **Observations** — Capture behaviours, concerns or changes you want to monitor.  
• **Symptoms** — Track symptoms and severity to understand patterns over time.  
• **Temperature Logs** — Monitor temperature alongside mood and energy levels.

Presented in a clean diary view with light and dark mode support, WellBee gives you a complete health history whenever you need it — especially when speaking with a healthcare professional.
