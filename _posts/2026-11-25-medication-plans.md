Keeping track of medication within a family can quickly become complicated, particularly when someone needs regular doses throughout the day. Knowing what was taken, what was missed and what’s coming up next can be difficult to manage from individual entries alone.

That’s why we’ve introduced dedicated Medication Plans in WellBee: Family Health Diary. They provide a clearer overview of recurring medication schedules, making it easier for parents to monitor doses and keep track of treatment over time.

## **Bringing medication schedules together**

In previous versions of WellBee, recurring medications could be scheduled as individual doses or multiple recurring doses. While this provided a way to manage upcoming doses, it didn’t offer a complete overview of how a medication plan was progressing.

That experience has now been improved.

Recurring medications now have their own dedicated Medication Plan overview, bringing together scheduled doses, taken and skipped entries, and overall progress in one place.

Parents can quickly see which doses have been taken, which have been skipped and how a medication schedule is progressing without having to work through individual entries.

## **More flexibility throughout the day**

Medication routines aren’t always straightforward. Some require just one dose a day, while others involve several doses spread across the day.

Medication Plans now support up to six scheduled doses per day, providing greater flexibility for families managing more involved medication routines.

Whether it’s a simple daily schedule or a more detailed routine, parents have a clearer way to organise and monitor recurring medications.

## **Making medication easier to manage**

These changes are about more than adding another way to schedule medications. They’re about giving parents a better understanding of what’s happening throughout a medication plan.

When someone in the family needs regular medication, having a clear record of taken and skipped doses can make it easier to stay organised, review progress and avoid relying on memory alone.

## **Continuing to improve WellBee**

Medication Plans are another step in WellBee’s ongoing development. The aim remains the same: to make managing family health a little easier by giving parents a straightforward way to record, organise and review the information that matters.

WellBee: Family Health Diary is available on the App Store, with its core features completely free to use.
